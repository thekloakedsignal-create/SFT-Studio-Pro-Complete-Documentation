Part Two

Understanding the Workspace

The SFT Studio Pro workspace is organized as a single calm surface: controls on the left, your work in the centre, and the queue watching over everything from below. This part tours each region so you always know where a control lives.

2.1  The Generation Panel

The generation panel, on the left of the workspace, is where every render begins. From top to bottom it contains the prompt field, the negative prompt field, the aspect ratio selector, the image count, the seed control, and the model and pipeline menus.

Every control in the panel shows a tooltip on hover, and every control is mirrored in Settings so you can change its default.

2.2  The Gallery

The gallery occupies the centre of the workspace. Every image you generate lands here, newest first. Click any thumbnail to open it in the viewer; hold Shift to select a range, or Ctrl (⌘) to select individual images for batch actions.

Right-clicking a thumbnail reveals the context menu: upscale, grade, use seed, use as keyframe, send to T2V, export, or delete. Nothing leaves the gallery until you delete it — clearing your queue never removes finished work.

2.3  The Queue

The queue runs along the bottom of the workspace. Every render job appears as a card with a live progress indicator, its prompt, and its settings. Jobs can be paused, resumed, reordered, or cancelled individually or as a group.

Tip. The queue survives restarts. If you close SFT Studio Pro with jobs running, it offers to resume them exactly where they stopped the next time you launch.

2.4  The Inspector

Select any image and the inspector opens on the right. It shows the full prompt, negative prompt, seed, model, aspect ratio, and pipeline used to create the image — plus any grading or upscaling applied since. The Use Settings button copies everything back into the generation panel, so any finished image can become the starting point for new work.

2.5  The Grading Bay

The grading bay is a dedicated stage for color work, described fully in Part Seven. It opens automatically when you choose Grade from the gallery, and it always works on a copy — your original render is never altered.

2.6  Saving and Loading Projects

A project bundles your prompt drafts, queue, gallery state, and settings into a single file. Save with Ctrl+S (⌘+S), and reopen from the welcome screen or with Ctrl+O. Projects are portable between machines as long as both installations are connected to the same provider account.
