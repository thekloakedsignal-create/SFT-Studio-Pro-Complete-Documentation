Aspect Ratios & Output Formats

Framing is a creative decision, not an afterthought. SFT Studio Pro ships with sixteen calibrated aspect ratios — every frame you need for social platforms, print, cinema, and everything between — each rendered natively rather than cropped after the fact.

4.2  Choosing the Right Frame

Start from where the image will live, not from habit. A platform list:

  • X 1.21:1 for in-stream posts, 16:9 for headers
  • Instagram 4:5 for feed, 1:1 for grid rows, 9:16 for Stories and Reels
  • YouTube 16:9 for thumbnails and end cards
  • Print 2:3 or 3:4 for covers and portraits, 5:4 for fine-art
  • Cinema & video 16:9 for standard, 1.85:1 and 2.39:1 for cinematic

4.3  Native Framing vs. Cropping

SFT Studio Pro composes every image inside its target ratio while it renders. A portrait composed natively at 2:3 places its subject deliberately; the same scene cropped from a 1:1 render is a salvage job. Choose the ratio before you generate, and let the model compose for the frame you actually need.

4.4  Resolution and Upscaling

Each ratio renders at a calibrated base resolution that balances speed, cost, and quality. When you need more pixels, select the image and choose Upscale → 2× or Upscale → 4×. Upscaled versions are saved as new files alongside the original; the original is never overwritten.

Tip. Generate at base resolution while you explore, and upscale only the keepers. A full batch at 4× costs time and credits you rarely need to spend.

4.5  File Formats

Renders are stored losslessly as PNG. On export you may choose PNG for maximum fidelity, or JPG with adjustable quality for lightweight delivery to the web. Format selection appears in the export dialog and is remembered per session.
