The Prompt Formula

Subject + Setting + Light + Style. Lead with what must not go wrong. Add one camera term if framing matters. Put exclusions in the negative prompt, not the prompt.

The Production Loop

  1. Prompt → batch of 4–8 → random seeds.
  2. Star the closest frame; lock its seed.
  3. Refine light and style; regenerate on the locked seed.
  4. Grade the keeper in the grading bay.
  5. Upscale, export, and move to the next frame.

Seeds in Three Rules

  • Seed -1 explores. A locked seed repeats.
  • Same prompt + same ratio + same seed = same image.
  • Every seed is recorded in the inspector. Trust it.

Essential Shortcuts

Ctrl/⌘ + G Generate  ·  Ctrl/⌘ + E Grade  ·  Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + E Export  ·  1–5 Rate  ·  Space Pause queue

The Grading Order

Exposure & contrast → white balance → HSL → split toning → vignette & grain. Judge the finished frame at fit-to-window.

The one rule above all. If a result matters, record its seed. Everything else in this manual can be looked up — a lost seed cannot.
